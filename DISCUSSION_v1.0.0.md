# v1.0.0: The FBA Reimbursement Engine is Live! 🚀

We are thrilled to announce the launch of **Verito v1.0.0**!

<img width="250" height="100" alt="Verito-logo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ba6d401-2fdf-4086-906d-196be5dd680f" />

Verito is designed to help Amazon FBA sellers stop leaking revenue by automating the tedious process of finding reimbursements. This release brings our core automated audit engine and evidence-gathering tools online, allowing sellers to recover lost funds while staying 100% compliant with Amazon's Terms of Service.

## ✨ Key Features in v1.0.0

### 🔄 Real-Time Reimbursement Tracking
* **Live Dashboard:** Monitor your reimbursement claims in real-time as they're processed
* **Claim Status Updates:** Get instant notifications on the status of each submitted claim
* **Recovery Metrics:** Track total funds recovered and pending reimbursements at a glance

### 🔍 Automated Claim Detection
* **Smart Discrepancy Detection:** Accurately identifies lost inventory, damaged goods, and overcharged fees automatically
* **Evidence Gathering:** Automatically compiles the exact data points and reports Amazon requires for successful claims
* **Zero Manual Effort:** Our engine does the heavy lifting—no more manual auditing

### 📦 Additional Features
* **Secure SP-API Integration:** Seamless OAuth connection to your Amazon Seller Central account
* **Automated Data Syncing:** Pulls and cross-references Inventory Ledger reports, inbound shipments, and fee data
* **Marketplace-Ready Packages:** Generates complete, easy-to-read claim packages ready for Seller Support
* **100% TOS Compliant Workflow:** No risky automated bots—we find the money and prepare the evidence; you simply review and submit
